the serious bit

My practice focuses on understanding and critiquing the impacts of emerging technologies on people, culture, and the planet. I'm particularly interested in working collectively to rethink the narratives and behaviours surrounding technologies — prioritising collaborative research as a process for testing, imagining and prototyping more equitable, alternative futures.
