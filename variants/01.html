I’m Kes (Kesia) Inkersole, a freelance creative strategist and internet equalities researcher based in London. My practice focuses on understanding and critiquing the impacts of emerging technologies on people, culture, and the planet. I work collectively to rethink the narratives and behaviours surrounding technologies, prioritising collaborative research as a process for testing, imagining and prototyping more equitable, alternative futures. I’m an associate lecturer at UAL (LCF + CCI), and I’ve worked with Neu Futur, DAZED, co—matter, MØRNING, WeTransfer, Protein, Midnight Agency, Softer and Selfridges. Below you’ll find projects for fun, projects for funds, some writing, some podcasts and a jungle machine. My CV and portfolio are on request, if you are curious about that...

KES FM 170.0 (jungle machine, for fun)
KES FM isn’t a radio station, it’s this website. I keep a jungle machine in here: the break is drummed out of oscillators and noise, recorded at 136bpm, sped up to 170 and chopped into slices, which is what the old samplers did to records. No samples, no files. Nothing plays until you press on air, and stop always stops it. While it’s on, look at your tab.
