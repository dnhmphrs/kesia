I’m Kes (Kesia) Inkersole, a freelance creative strategist and internet equalities researcher based in London. My practice focuses on understanding and critiquing the impacts of emerging technologies on people, culture, and the planet. I’m particularly interested in working collectively to rethink the narratives and behaviours surrounding technologies, prioritising collaborative research as a process for testing, imagining and prototyping more equitable, alternative futures. I’m an associate lecturer at UAL (LCF and CCI), and I’ve collaborated with Neu Futur, DAZED, co—matter, MØRNING, WeTransfer, Protein, Midnight Agency, Softer and Selfridges. Below you’ll find a selection of experiments (for fun), commissions (for funds), writing, conversations and one jungle machine. There’s no CV spreadsheet here, but there is a CV and portfolio on request if you are curious about that: inkersolekesia@gmail.com. Otherwise, continue reading...

KES FM 170.0 (jungle machine, for fun)

KES FM isn’t a real station, it’s this website. Every drum and bassline is synthesised live in your browser from oscillators and noise: no samples, no files, nothing uploaded, nothing kept. The break is recorded at 136 and played back at 170, which is what the old samplers did to records, then chopped into slices you can rearrange. Nothing plays until you press ▶ on air, and ■ stop always stops it.
