i'm a freelance creative strategist and internet equalities researcher in london. my practice focuses on understanding and critiquing the impacts of emerging technologies on people, culture, and the planet. i work collectively to rethink the narratives and behaviours surrounding technologies, prioritising collaborative research as a process for testing, imagining and prototyping more equitable, alternative futures.

i'm also an associate lecturer at UAL (LCF + CCI), and i've worked with Neu Futur, DAZED, co⁠—⁠matter, MØRNING, WeTransfer, Protein, Midnight Agency, Softer and Selfridges.
