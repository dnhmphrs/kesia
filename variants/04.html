Kesia Inkersole is a freelance creative strategist and internet equalities researcher based in London. Her practice focuses on understanding and critiquing the impacts of emerging technologies on people, culture and the planet, prioritising collaborative research as a process for testing, imagining and prototyping more equitable, alternative futures. She is an associate lecturer at UAL (London College of Fashion and the Creative Computing Institute) and has worked with Neu Futur, DAZED, co⁠—⁠matter, MØRNING, WeTransfer, Protein, Midnight Agency, Softer and Selfridges.
