I’m Kes (Kesia) Inkersole, a freelance creative strategist and internet equalities researcher based in London. My practice focuses on understanding and critiquing the impacts of emerging technologies on people, culture, and the planet. I work collectively to rethink the narratives and behaviours surrounding technologies, prioritising collaborative research as a process for testing, imagining and prototyping more equitable, alternative futures. I’m an associate lecturer at UAL (LCF + CCI), and I’ve worked with Neu Futur, DAZED, co—matter, MØRNING, WeTransfer, Protein, Midnight Agency, Softer and Selfridges. Below you’ll find experiments (for fun), commissions (for funds), writing, conversations, and the rooms I’ve taught and talked in. I’m available for work, so write to inkersolekesia@gmail.com. Otherwise, keep scrolling: there is a jungle machine at the bottom of the page, and it won’t make a sound until you ask it to.
