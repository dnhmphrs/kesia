liner notes

jungle came out of london in the early 90s, out of Black British sound system culture: reggae and dub basslines, MCs, dubplates and rewinds, colliding with breakbeat hardcore at 160-something bpm. pirate radio played it long before the official stations would. this page borrows its look and its language, so it should say whose they are.

the drums are amen-shaped. the amen break is a few seconds of gregory c. coleman drumming on "amen, brother" by the winstons (1969), and it's one of the most sampled recordings ever made. the band never got royalties for any of that. so the machine doesn't sample him: every hit is synthesised from scratch, in your browser.

(the internet equalities bit: pirate radio was people bolting aerials to tower block roofs because the official airwaves wouldn't carry their music. a network built by the people the official one left out. that's still what a lot of the best internet looks like.)
