I’m Kes (Kesia) Inkersole, a freelance creative strategist and internet equalities researcher based in London. My practice focuses on understanding and critiquing the impacts of emerging technologies on people, culture, and the planet. I’m particularly interested in working collectively to rethink the narratives and behaviours surrounding technologies, prioritising collaborative research as a process for testing, imagining and prototyping more equitable, alternative futures. I’m an associate lecturer at UAL (LCF + CCI), and I’ve collaborated with Neu Futur, DAZED, co—matter, MØRNING, WeTransfer, Protein, Midnight Agency, Softer and Selfridges, among others. Below you’ll find a selection of projects, some for fun and some for funds, then writing, podcasts, talks and teaching. My CV and portfolio are on request, so email me if you are curious about that. KES FM 170.0 (jungle machine)

Also for fun. KES FM isn’t a real station, it’s this website: a jungle machine running in your browser, every break and bassline synthesised live from oscillators and noise, no samples, no files. Nothing plays until you press ▶ on air, and ■ stop always stops it. It asks before it makes a noise, which is more than can be said for most of the internet.
