text in your shout-outs: inkersolekesia@gmail.com

Kes here (Kesia on forms). I'm a freelance creative strategist and internet equalities researcher in London, and an associate lecturer at UAL, at LCF and the CCI. I've worked with Neu Futur, DAZED, co—matter, MØRNING, WeTransfer, Protein, Midnight Agency, Softer and Selfridges. I'm available for work: inkersolekesia@gmail.com. CV and portfolio on request.

The serious bit: my practice focuses on understanding and critiquing the impacts of emerging technologies on people, culture, and the planet. I'm particularly interested in working collectively to rethink the narratives and behaviours surrounding technologies, prioritising collaborative research as a process for testing, imagining and prototyping more equitable, alternative futures.
