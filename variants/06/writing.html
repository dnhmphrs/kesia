chainmail ♡

short pieces, the kind written in a notes app. the plan is simple: a quick email goes out, and the piece pops up here, newest first, collecting over time into a kind of text moodboard.

how it works

pieces arrive by email at (address tbc). the subject line becomes the title, the date is the day it was sent.

for now the dates and the words are placeholders.
