I’m Kes Inkersole, a creative strategist and internet equalities researcher based in London. My practice focuses on understanding and critiquing the impacts of emerging technologies on people, culture, and the planet. I work collectively to rethink the narratives and behaviours surrounding technologies, prioritising collaborative research as a process for testing, imagining and prototyping more equitable, alternative futures. I’m an associate lecturer at UAL (LCF + CCI), and I’ve worked with Neu Futur, DAZED, co—matter, MØRNING, WeTransfer, Protein, Midnight Agency, Softer and Selfridges. Below you’ll find some projects for fun, some for funds, and some writing and talking. There is no CV spreadsheet: my CV and portfolio are on request, by email, if you are curious about that. KES FM 170.0 (jungle machine)

This is not a radio station, it’s this website: a small jungle machine running in your browser. The break is drummed out of oscillators and noise, recorded at 136bpm, played back at 170 and chopped into slices, which is what the old samplers did to records. No samples, no files. Nothing plays until you press on air, and stop always stops it.
