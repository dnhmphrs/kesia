liner notes ☆

this page borrows from jungle (the breaks, the pirate radio idents, the flyers, the tape packs), so it should say where they come from.

jungle came out of london in the early 90s: Black British sound system culture (reggae, dub, dancehall, the soundclash) meeting rave and breakbeat hardcore, carried across the city by pirate radio.

the amen break is four bars of drumming by G.C. Coleman of The Winstons, from their 1969 b-side "Amen, Brother". it's one of the most sampled recordings ever made, and the band never got royalties for it. the machine here doesn't use a sample, it rebuilds the pattern from synthesised hits, but the groove is still his. big up G.C. Coleman.

which is the internet equalities question too: who made the thing, who gets the credit, who gets paid.
