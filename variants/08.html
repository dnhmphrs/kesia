KES FM is not a real station (hi, Ofcom). It's this website: a little jungle machine in your browser, every drum and bassline synthesised live, no samples, no files. It makes no sound until you press on air, and stop always stops it. While it's on it plays the page: each slice of the break is a row in the table below, so you can watch my work get chopped.

I'm Kes, Kesia on paper. My practice focuses on understanding and critiquing the impacts of emerging technologies on people, culture, and the planet. I'm particularly interested in working collectively to rethink the narratives and behaviours surrounding technologies, prioritising collaborative research as a process for testing, imagining and prototyping more equitable, alternative futures.

I'm an associate lecturer at UAL (LCF + CCI), and I freelance. I've worked with Neu Futur, DAZED, co—matter, MØRNING, WeTransfer, Protein, Midnight Agency, Softer and Selfridges. CV and portfolio on request: inkersolekesia@gmail.com.
