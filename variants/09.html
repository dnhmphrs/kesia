I’m Kes (Kesia) Inkersole, a freelance creative strategist and internet equalities researcher based in London. My practice focuses on understanding and critiquing the impacts of emerging technologies on people, culture, and the planet. I work collectively to rethink the narratives and behaviours surrounding technologies, prioritising collaborative research as a process for testing, imagining and prototyping more equitable, alternative futures. I’m an associate lecturer at UAL (LCF + CCI), and I have worked with Neu Futur, DAZED, co—matter, MØRNING, WeTransfer, Protein, Midnight Agency, Softer and Selfridges. Below you’ll find some projects for fun, some for funds, and some writing, podcasts and teaching. My CV and portfolio are on request, if you are curious about that: inkersolekesia@gmail.com. There is also a jungle machine further down; it won’t make a sound unless you ask it to. Otherwise, continue reading...
