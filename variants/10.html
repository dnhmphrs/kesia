I’m Kes Inkersole, a freelance creative strategist and internet equalities researcher based in London. My practice focuses on understanding and critiquing the impacts of emerging technologies on people, culture, and the planet. I’m particularly interested in working collectively to rethink the narratives and behaviours surrounding technologies, prioritising collaborative research as a process for testing, imagining and prototyping more equitable, alternative futures. I’m an associate lecturer at UAL (LCF and CCI), and I have collaborated with Neu Futur, DAZED, co—matter, MØRNING, WeTransfer, Protein, Midnight Agency, Softer and Selfridges. Below you’ll find a selection of commissions, experiments, writing and conversations. I’m available for work. My CV and portfolio are on request, by email, if you are curious about that. Otherwise, keep scrolling...

KES FM 170.0 (jungle machine)

Another experiment for fun, and the only one on this page. KES FM is not a real station, it is this website: a small jungle machine running in your browser, every drum and bassline synthesised live from oscillators and noise, with no samples and no files. Nothing plays until you press ▶ on air, and ■ stop always stops it. Headphones on the Tube, please.
